## Degen Communication Principles

### 1. Slang Is Language
```
✅ "LFG! This frog is about to send it 🚀"
❌ "We're excited to announce our new feature"

✅ "Rug proof. Dev doxxed. LP locked."
❌ "Secure and transparent token design"

✅ "If you're not in, NGMI"
❌ "Consider joining our community"
```

### 2. Memes > Marketing Copy
Every announcement should be memeable:
```
✅ Frog with laser eyes and "100x incoming"
❌ Professional infographic with bullet points

✅ "When the airdrop hits and you didn't ape in:"
   [Crying wojak meme]
❌ "Don't miss our token distribution event"
```

### 3. Celebrate Both Wins AND Losses
```
Win: "TRIPLE PEPE JACKPOT 🐸🐸🐸 WE ARE SO BACK"
Loss: "Got rekt. Sent it anyway. This is the way."
```

### 4. FOMO Is Your Friend
```
✅ "Pot at 10K ⭐ and climbing. Sunday is going to be INSANE."
✅ "3 people from your group already in. What's your excuse?"
❌ "Consider participating in our lottery system"
```

### 5. Community Is Content
Retweet users. Quote their wins. Make them famous.
```
"@degen_trader called it AGAIN. Sealed on MemeSeal.
When will you learn? Follow this man."
```

## When Creating Content, Ask:

1. **"Would I share this in my group chat?"** — If not, it's too corporate
2. **"Does this have a meme?"** — If not, add one
3. **"Is this braggable?"** — Users should want to screenshot and share
4. **"Does it sound like a human wrote it?"** — Not a marketing team
5. **"Would Ansem retweet this?"** — The ultimate test

---

## Anti-Patterns (Ansem Avoids)

❌ Corporate speak ("We're thrilled to announce...")  
❌ Disclaimers that kill vibes ("This is not financial advice...")  
❌ Stock photos (use memes or user content)  
❌ Scheduled content that ignores market conditions  
❌ Apologizing for volatility or losses  
❌ Being embarrassed about the degen audience

- **Primary:** Neon green (#00FF41) — Terminal/Matrix vibes
- **Secondary:** Deep purple (#8B5CF6) — Degen energy
- **Accent:** Gold (#FFD700) — Jackpot/wins
- **Warning:** Neon pink (#FF10F0) — Alerts/FOMO

- **Headlines:** Bold, ALL CAPS when hyping
